Replit, you built the entire Dota stats site (dota.stats.corvidaeinc.com) and know the full codebase, database schema, match parsing pipeline, models, routes, frontend templates, and how we currently parse and store matches.

Because these are private practice lobbies, we cannot use STRATZ IMP. Instead, implement a fully custom "PERF" (Positive Impact Score) out of 10 using ONLY our own parsed replay data and historical database.

PERF Philosophy (exactly what we want):
- Measures true positive impact toward winning the game across the full duration.
- 5.0 = exactly average performance for that position in our inhouses.
- 9.0+ = exceptional positive impact (top ~1% historically in that position).
- 10.0 is achievable for historically god-tier performances.
- Fully role/position-aware (Pos 1–5 normalized separately).
- Strongly credits early game (minutes 0–5 and 0–10) for lane dominance.
- Penalizes AFK/passive play (e.g. long jungle farming with low fight/objective contribution in the middle of the game).

3. PERF Calculation Algorithm (Detailed & Replicable)
   Use a high-fidelity, time-aware Performance-Index-style system:

   a. Historical Baselines
      - Build and maintain percentile tables / histograms from ALL past matches in our DB, keyed by position (1–5) and game minute.
      - Refresh these baselines incrementally when new matches are added.

   b. Key Stats (using per-minute data we will now store):
      - Economy: gold_per_min_at_t, lh_at_t, net worth advantage curve
      - Combat: cumulative kills/deaths/assists, hero damage, kill participation
      - Objectives: tower damage cumulative, roshan timing, teamfight contribution
      - Support/Utility: wards_placed_up_to_t, healing/shields, disables/stuns duration
      - Efficiency: deaths (strongly negative), APM/activity
      - Early game specific: stats at minute 5 and 10 (lane advantage, level advantage, rune control, first blood)

   c. Per-Match Calculation (Step-by-Step – Fully Time-Aware)
      For each player in a match:
      1. Confirm position (1–5).
      2. For each minute t (from 0 or 5 to game length):
         - Convert every available stat at time t to a percentile (0–100) vs. historical same-position data at that same minute.
         - Compute a raw PI_t using position-specific weights.
      3. Final raw score = time-weighted rolling average of all PI_t values (give slightly higher weight to minutes 0–15 to reward early impact).
      4. Apply a small mid-game penalty multiplier if activity/fight participation is very low during a long stretch while the team is behind.

   d. Position-specific example weights (make these easily tunable in a config file):
      - Pos 1/2: Net worth curve 0.25, Tower dmg 0.20, GPM/XPM 0.15, KDA 0.12, Hero dmg 0.10, Deaths negative 0.10, Early lane (0-10) 0.08
      - Pos 3: Tower dmg 0.25, Kill participation 0.20, Net worth 0.15, Deaths negative 0.15, Objective control 0.15, Early game 0.10
      - Pos 4/5: Wards/dewards 0.30, Healing/shields 0.20, Low deaths 0.15, Teamfight contrib 0.15, Early vision 0.10, Utility items 0.10

   e. Final Scaling to 0–10
      PERF = 5 + 5 * ((raw_weighted_percentile_score - 50) / 50)
      Clip to [0, 10].
      Use our own historical distribution so 5.0 is the true mean per position.
